# Keeping and shredding paper

PaperJay keeps every document's digital copy for good. The paper is another matter: most of it can go
after a while. PaperJay tells you when.

## How long each paper is kept

Every document has a **Keep the paper** line on its page: "Until Dec 31, 2031" or "For good", and the rule
that says so. The rules are in **Settings → Keeping paper**. These are the ones PaperJay starts with:

| Rule | Keep the paper |
|---|---|
| Contracts, IDs, deeds, investments and medical records | for good |
| Tax returns and the papers behind them (W-2, 1099, donations, child care…) | 7 years after the end of its year |
| Insurance policies and cards | 1 year after it expires |
| Medical bills and receipts | 3 years after the end of its year |
| Pay slips | 1 year after the end of its year |
| Bank and credit card statements | 1 year after the end of its year |
| Utility and phone bills | 1 year after the end of its year |
| Shop receipts | 1 year after the end of its year |

The first rule that matches a document decides. A rule matches by **category** (medical, insurance,
utilities, shopping, home, vehicle, tax, income, travel, food, finance), **kind** of document (receipt,
invoice, bill, statement, insurance, tax, medical, pay, contract, id, letter) or **topic** (the topics in
Browse). **A document no rule matches is kept for good**: PaperJay never suggests shredding a paper it can't
place.

These defaults follow common advice for households in the US. What you must keep depends on where you live
and on your situation (a business, a house you'll sell, an open tax question), so check with your tax office
or adviser and change the rules to fit: change the years, what each rule covers and their order, add your
own, or go back to the defaults.

To decide for one document, change **Keep the paper** on its page: keep it for good, until a date, or back
to the rules.

## Paper to shred

**Settings → Paper to shred** (the start page shows it too when there is some) lists the papers on your
paper shelf (Settings → Paper shelf: number the paper you keep) that have been kept long enough, with their numbers. Take them off the shelf, shred
them, and tap **Shredded** (or **All shredded**). **Keep it** keeps a paper for good instead.

- Only the paper is affected. The document, its file and its searchable PDF stay in PaperJay.
- A shredded paper's number is freed on the shelf but never given to another paper, so an old label can't be
  confused with a new one. The document's page says when it was shredded and the number it had, with Undo.
- Papers without a number aren't on the list unless you tick **Also documents without a number on the
  paper shelf**: PaperJay can't know whether you kept them.
